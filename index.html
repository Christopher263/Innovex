The problem

Nobody loses this revenue on purpose. It just goes quiet.

The proposal black hole

You send it, feel good about it, and move on to the next client. Three weeks later someone asks "did we ever hear back from them?" — and nobody knows.

Unpaid invoice drift

An invoice goes out, a client goes quiet, and chasing it feels awkward enough that it keeps sliding to next week — for weeks. That's cash you already earned.

Memory-based ops

"Who's supposed to follow up on this?" only has an answer as long as the one person who remembers is around, paying attention, and not buried in three other client fires.

If any of that sounds familiar, the fix isn't more discipline — it's taking it off a person's memory entirely.

FAQ

Questions firms actually ask us

Does this replace our CRM?

No. Innovex isn't a CRM — it doesn't do lead scoring or deal notes. It's focused on the money already sitting in your pipeline: proposals waiting on a signature, invoices waiting on payment.

Will the follow-ups sound like spam?

They shouldn't — they send from your real inbox, in a cadence and tone you set, and stop the moment a client replies, signs, or pays. You review and approve the sequence copy before it ever goes out.

Can I approve every message before it sends?

Yes — that's the default. Every drafted follow-up lands in an approval queue first. You read it, edit it if you want, and click approve before anything reaches your client. You can turn on set-and-forget sending later, per sequence, once you trust the drafts.

Do we have to give you access to our whole inbox?

No. You can forward specific proposal or invoice threads to your Innovex address instead of connecting an inbox at all.

How long does setup actually take?

Most firms are connected and seeing their first board of open proposals and invoices within an hour. There's no data migration and nothing to import — Innovex reads what's already in your inbox.

What happens after a client replies or pays?

The sequence for that thread stops immediately — nobody gets a follow-up after they've already responded. A signed proposal or paid invoice moves to "Signed / Paid" on your board and counts toward your recovered-revenue reporting.

We're smaller than $1M, or bigger than $20M — still worth talking?

We're building specifically for firms in that range — small enough that one stalled invoice matters, big enough that you can't chase every one by hand. Reach out anyway; we're still shaping who this fits.

What does “design partner” pricing actually mean?

We're pre-launch and working closely with a small group of firms to build the product. Design partners get the rate on this page locked for 12 months, direct input on the roadmap, and white-glove setup — in exchange for feedback and a short case study once we see results.
